Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

This policy covers the leonific uploader, a private tool the owner of the leonific YouTube channel uses to publish its own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload), to upload approved videos.
  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl), to set the title, description, schedule, thumbnail and synthetic-content label of those videos, and to read the channel name to confirm the sign-in.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The tool reads and writes only the channel owner's own videos. It does not read viewers' data, comments or subscriber lists.
  • No data obtained from Google is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of YouTube follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it makes the stored token useless; the owner can also delete it from their computer.
