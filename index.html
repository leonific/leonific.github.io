How the videos are made

Each Short is scripted, illustrated and narrated with AI tools, then checked and approved by a person before it is published. Videos are labelled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube. Characters in dramatised scenes are fictional unless stated otherwise.

The leonific uploader

The leonific uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload approved videos to the leonific channel and schedule when they go live. It is not offered to other users.
